3. Personal Data & Kundali Information

Glow Date provides personalized astrological calculations (Kundali / Horoscope).

  • On-Device Storage: Any names, birth dates, birth times, and birth locations entered for Kundali generation are stored strictly locally on your device within an encrypted/sandboxed local database (SQLite/Room).
  • No Cloud Upload: Your birth details and generated charts are never transmitted to our servers or any cloud database.
  • User Control: You have complete control to add, view, hide, or permanently delete saved Kundalis directly within the app at any time.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Glow Date does not store your personal information on external servers:
