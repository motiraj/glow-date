## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **Glow Date** ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play. We are committed to transparency and respect your privacy. Glow Date is designed so that your personal astrological data and dates remain securely on your own device.

---

## 2. Permissions Requested & Purpose

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, Glow Date only requests permissions required for core functionality:

| Permission | Category | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`<br>`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` | Location | **High-Precision Vedic Astronomy Calculations:** Calculations of local sunrise, sunset, Tithi endings, Choghadiya, Hora, and Gochar (planetary transits) depend strictly on your geographic latitude and longitude. Coordinates are calculated purely on-device and are **never** stored on external servers or tracked in the background. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Notifications (Android 13+) | To provide daily Vedic Panchanga reminders, festival alerts, and Ekadashi notifications. You can turn notifications on or off at any time. |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Device Startup | To re-register scheduled local notification alarms after your device is rebooted. |
| `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Network | To serve advertisements (via Google AdMob), resolve city names if reverse geocoding is selected, and check network availability. |

---

## 3. Personal Data & Kundali Storage
- **100% On-Device Processing:** Any personal information you enter into the app—including name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, and birthplace for Kundali / Horoscope creation—is saved exclusively in your device's local database (SQLite/Room).
- **No Cloud Upload:** None of your personal horoscope details, saved profiles, or notes are uploaded to any cloud server or shared with third parties.
- **User Control:** You can view, hide, or permanently delete saved Kundalis from the app at any time.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because Glow Date does not transmit or store your personal data on remote servers:
- All data resides strictly on your physical device.
- You can completely erase all app data at any time by navigating to:  
  **Phone Settings > Apps > Glow Date > Storage > Clear Data**, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 6. Security
We take security seriously. All computations are performed client-side using native algorithms. We rely on the Android sandboxing security model to isolate app data from other applications on your device.
